# SPEC-0002: Kids' Routine Board

## Graph Edges

- **Implements:** [ADR-0003](../../../adrs/ADR-0003-kid-access-via-device-accounts.md) — kid actions run under enrolled device accounts
- **Requires:** [SPEC-0001](../household-platform/spec.md) — members, kid access on enrolled devices, sync, security baseline
- **Requires:** [SPEC-0006](../household-assistant/spec.md) — approved mascot lines generated by the household assistant

## Overview

Each kid gets a board of today's tasks as big tiles to tick off, with a household mascot that cheers them on. The
board is deliberately free of menus and settings: a kid sees what is due today, taps it when done, and gets feedback.
Routine items also cover parents' recurring chores (shown in SPEC-0003) and the kids' hobbies (shown in SPEC-0004).

**v1 scope** (SPEC-0001 "Version 1 Scope"): everything in this spec; the mascot uses only the built-in line pool until
the household assistant (SPEC-0006) exists.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Routine Items

A routine item SHALL have an emoji, a title, an optional hint, an optional time of day, a kind (`chore` or `hobby`),
the weekdays it occurs on, and an assignment. The assignment MUST be one of: a specific kid, both kids, kids
alternating, a specific parent, or both parents. Only parents MAY create, edit or delete routine items.

#### Scenario: Soccer twice a week

- **WHEN** a parent creates "⚽ Fußballtraining", kind hobby, Tuesday and Thursday, 17:00, assigned to the son
- **THEN** the son's board shows the tile on Tuesdays and Thursdays only, labelled "17:00 Uhr"

#### Scenario: Kid cannot edit routines

- **WHEN** a kid session tries to change a routine item's weekdays
- **THEN** the server MUST reject the change

### Requirement: Routine Management

Parents MUST have a screen listing all routine items where they can add, edit and delete items, setting emoji, title,
hint, an optional time of day (HH:MM), kind, weekdays and assignment (the routines screen is `#/routinen`, the mascot picker
`#/maskottchen`). Changes MUST apply from the next time the boards render; ticks already recorded MUST be kept when a
routine is edited or moved, and are removed together with the routine when it is deleted.

#### Scenario: Moving piano to Wednesday

- **WHEN** a parent changes "Klavierstunde" from Tuesday to Wednesday
- **THEN** it disappears from Tuesday's board and appears on Wednesday's, and last Tuesday's tick stays in the week strip

#### Scenario: Deleting a routine

- **WHEN** a parent deletes a routine that has ticks
- **THEN** the routine and its own ticks are removed and other routines' ticks are untouched

### Requirement: Alternating Assignment

An item assigned to "kids alternating" MUST be due for exactly one kid per occurring day, determined
deterministically from the date so every device agrees without coordination. The tile MUST show that it is this kid's
turn ("Heute du").

#### Scenario: Dishwasher alternates

- **WHEN** "Spülmaschine aus- & einräumen" occurs every day and is assigned to kids alternating
- **THEN** on any given day it appears on exactly one kid's board, and the next day on the other's

### Requirement: Today Board

A kid's board MUST show every routine item due for that kid today as a tile, a progress indicator (done / total), and
the kid's name and weekday. A single tap MUST toggle the tile between done and not done for today. Ticks MUST be stored
per member, per item, per day. A day with no due items MUST show a "free day" message instead of an empty grid.

#### Scenario: Ticking homework

- **WHEN** the daughter taps "Hausaufgaben" on her board
- **THEN** the tile shows as done, the progress goes from 0/5 to 1/5, and a parent's view shows it done within the
  live-sync window

#### Scenario: Undoing a mistaken tick

- **WHEN** a kid taps an already-done tile the same day
- **THEN** it returns to not done without any celebration

### Requirement: Tomorrow Preview

The board SHOULD show tomorrow's hobby items below today's tiles, with their hints, so kids can prepare (for example,
pack riding boots the evening before).

#### Scenario: Riding tomorrow

- **WHEN** the daughter has "Reiten" tomorrow with hint "Reithelm & Stiefel"
- **THEN** her board today shows a "Morgen" note with that item and hint

### Requirement: Week Strip and Streak

The board MUST show the current week (Monday to Sunday) with a marker per day: all done, partly done, nothing done, or
no items. It MUST show a streak: the number of consecutive days, ending today or yesterday, on which all due items were
done. Days without due items MUST NOT break or extend the streak. Today MUST count once it is complete.

#### Scenario: Weekend without tasks

- **WHEN** a kid finished everything Thursday and Friday, had no items Saturday, and finished everything Sunday
- **THEN** the streak on Sunday is 3

### Requirement: Completion Celebration

When the last due item of the day is ticked, the board MUST show a celebration (full-screen message from the mascot,
confetti) that the kid dismisses with one tap. Animations MUST be suppressed when the device requests reduced motion.

#### Scenario: Last tile

- **WHEN** the son ticks his last open tile
- **THEN** the mascot's "Alles geschafft!" celebration appears and is spoken if sound is on

### Requirement: Mascot Feedback

The household mascot (an emoji animal with a name chosen by the family) MUST react visually to every tick. It SHALL
speak a line aloud in German, using the device's speech synthesis, on the first tick of the day (kept per device and kid),
on completion, and otherwise rarely: never straight after it has spoken, with a one in four chance on later ticks, and
always after three silent ticks in a row. It MUST NOT speak on every tick. The opening greeting is shown but not spoken
(speech is tied to ticks); tapping the mascot itself speaks a line. Sound MUST be switchable per device.
Lines MUST come from the approved lines for that kid and day (SPEC-0006); when none are approved, the board MUST use the
built-in fallback pool.

#### Scenario: Occasional voice

- **WHEN** a kid ticks five tiles in a row
- **THEN** the mascot speaks on the first tick, stays silent on at least one of the next three, and speaks on the last

#### Scenario: No approved lines

- **WHEN** the assistant produced no approved lines for today
- **THEN** the mascot uses the built-in fallback lines and the board works normally

### Requirement: Mascot Choice

Parents MUST be able to set the mascot's animal and name for the household. The choice SHOULD be made by the kids;
the UI SHALL offer at least eight animals.

#### Scenario: Kids rename the mascot

- **WHEN** a parent sets the mascot to 🦊 "Fuchsi"
- **THEN** every board shows the fox with that name in its speech bubble

### Requirement: No Competitive or Reward Mechanics by Default

The board MUST NOT compare siblings, show rankings, or award points or prizes unless parents explicitly enable such a
feature in a future spec.

#### Scenario: Two boards side by side

- **WHEN** both kids' boards are open on two iPads
- **THEN** neither board shows the other kid's progress

## Security Requirements

Per SPEC-0001 § "Security Requirements → Authentication": every endpoint this capability uses is Auth: Required; kid

Per SPEC-0001 § "Security Requirements → Rate Limiting". No deviation in this capability.

Per SPEC-0001 § "Security Requirements → Security Headers". No deviation in this capability.

Per SPEC-0001 § "Security Requirements → Request Body Size Limits" (64 KB default). No deviation in this capability.

### CSRF Protection

Per SPEC-0001 § "Security Requirements → CSRF Protection" (header tokens, no auth cookies). No deviation in this capability.

### Redirect Validation

Per SPEC-0001 § "Security Requirements → Redirect Validation". This capability performs no redirects.

## Accessibility Requirements

This spec involves user-facing UI. The following are MANDATORY for every UI component this spec produces, per WCAG 2.1 AA:

- **WCAG 2.1 AA compliance** — the minimum conformance target
- **ARIA landmarks** — `role="banner"`, `role="navigation"`, `role="main"`, `role="contentinfo"` on page-structure elements
- **`aria-label` on icon-only controls** — every button or link with no visible text label
- **`aria-live` regions for dynamic content** — `polite` for routine updates, `assertive` for critical status changes (HTMX swaps, auto-refresh panels, real-time status)
- **Keyboard navigation** — logical tab order, Enter/Space activation, Escape to dismiss, arrow keys within composite widgets
- **Focus management in modals and dialogs** — trap focus while open, move focus to the first focusable element on open, return it to the trigger on close

The full checklist behind each line is the SDD plugin's `references/accessibility-requirements.md`; this section is the normative summary and is not expanded inline.
